Miracles Star Neon patch - Reverse Direction

Adds a Reverse Direction button to the plugin widget.
Protocol frames are based on the uploaded GlowBridge source:
  Forward: BC 07 01 01 55
  Reverse: BC 07 01 00 55

The button toggles direction and sends the direction command immediately.
Whenever a hardware effect is selected, the plugin also reapplies the current
 direction after the effect-selection frame, matching GlowBridge's two-frame
 effect sequence. The existing named and numeric effect modes are retained.

Replace NeonController.h, NeonController.cpp, and MiraclesStarNeonPlugin.cpp
in D:\\MiraclesStarNeon, then rebuild with the user's existing qmake/nmake steps.
This patch has not been compiled or tested against the physical strip here.
